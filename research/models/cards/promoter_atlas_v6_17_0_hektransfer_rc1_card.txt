## English

batch22 evaluates frozen K562 representations for HEK293 core-promoter transfer. Of 676 GSE188639 members (Zhao et al. 2023), 673 join by exact prom_id to hg19 133 bp sequences from Haberle et al. 2019 Supplementary Table 6. SCP1_mTATA/mDPE/mboth were excluded because public sequences were absent.

The target hek_norm is `log2(hek exp/input)`, matching published normalization within 8.88e-16; negative values are retained. Measured K562 values support paired-cell diagnostics. Forward/RC Hamming≤16 and same-locus overlap give 673 rows/669 groups, five folds with seed20261025, and zero cross-fold grouping violations. Four rows exactly match a K562 pretraining 133-mer and are stratified.

The frozen source is batch21 RecipeA ckpt_A_seed20261023, SHA excerpt bc35e5585fa7..., using 128-dimensional concatenated mean/max representations on cuda:0, batch32, over complete native inserts. R0 is the outer-TRAIN mean; R1 is 86-dimensional mono4+di16+tri64+GC+log1p length Ridge(alpha100); R2 adds the 128-dimensional representation for 214 features. Five folds and two recipes completed ten ridge fits.

| Recipe | OOF rho | MAE | R² | Top10 | Enrichment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| R0 | -0.0891 | 0.6527 | -0.0023 | 0.1029 | 1.02 |
| R1 | 0.4566 | 0.5717 | 0.1915 | 0.2941 | 2.91 |
| R2 | 0.4617 | 0.5946 | 0.1939 | 0.3088 | 3.06 |

Criteria required rho gain≥0.03, MAE reduction≥10%, stable/improved top10, and positive rho gain in at least four of five folds; a CI crossing zero was uncertain. Observed gain was 0.0051, MAE reduction −4.0%, three positive folds, and CI lower≤0, falling short of practical gain. RTX5060 Laptop extraction took about 0.019ms/sequence within a 30-minute GPU budget.

This card records retrospective grouped CV over 673 public HEK293 sequences. batch23 corrected this batch's earlier assessment that local construct sequences were absent; see the next card for sequence availability and assay mapping. Artifacts are in batch22 reports/data/code directories; raw-input CLI is b22_cli.py.
