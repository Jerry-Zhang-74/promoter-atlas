## English

batch16 compared human gene-level natural-expression E/S/B models and selected independent arm A. Three independent models use four-slot gene-bag ACGT one-hot CNN inputs (N→zero) plus a frozen 1069-dimensional composition/position branch.

E is Score_E in [0,1], S is detectable-tissue `-MAD(log2(1+TPM))` with 18769 S_defined genes, and B is detectable-tissue fraction. Validation selection requires mean per-axis rho gain≥0.02 and each axis drop≤0.02. Recorded mean_gain=-0.006075287767231541, no_drop_gt_0.02=true, rule_pass=false; the independent composite was retained.

Deployment manifest: `models/breakthrough_20261002/batch16/deployment_bundle.json`; E/S/B weights are in `A_s20261016/model_E.pt`, model_S.pt, and model_B.pt.

Matched HGB validation/test rho: E45 0.7302/0.7335, S1069 0.4362/0.4334, B45 0.7097/0.7191. Evaluation is retrospective with reused TEST exposure. Grouping covers discovered relationship edges, with broader homology coverage remaining to be assessed.
