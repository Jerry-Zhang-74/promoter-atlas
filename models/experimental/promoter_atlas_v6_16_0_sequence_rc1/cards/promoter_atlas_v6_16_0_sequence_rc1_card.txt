## English

batch20, dated 2026-10-03, adds raw-input and gene-catalog entry points dispatching two existing model types.

### Models and replay

The gene model uses the three independent SingleTaskModels from v6.15.1-release.rc2, seed20261016, with 4×400 bp encoded sequences and 1069 raw features. E is Score_E, S is detectable-tissue negative MAD, and B is detectable-tissue fraction. SHA excerpts: E `9f33f8e6..cab79`, S `2e5fff25..4d214`, B `c45af034..33aeb`.

The K562 model averages 15 LegNet-inspired weights from five family folds and three seeds. Input is 164 bp; output is GSE118242 Mean_log10 activity. ensemble_sd describes model spread. Full hashes are in batch20/artifact_manifest.json.

The gene corrected-component split has TEST n5851 and val n5609. Independent replay E/S/B is 0.74279/0.43418/0.72618 versus rc2 0.7427954/0.4341868/0.7261787, differing by ≤7e-6. Raw-bag feature recomputation has maximum difference 0. Fold-aware MPRA OOF replay over 40 oligos differs by at most 8e-8.

### Metrics and usage domain

Random-split MPRA TEST gives rho0.94129 and MAE0.0768; 81.4% of TEST rows share family_id with TRAIN. The grouped OOF reference is rho0.79577 and MAE0.16872. Its gain over CNN0.78285 falls below the prespecified robustness margin.

MPRA batch32 GPU latency is about 0.55ms/sequence; gene batch64 is 1.33ms/gene and single-gene calls about 2.4–2.9ms. CPU supports small inputs. Query catalog genes by ID/symbol. Out-of-catalog bags use a first-min(4,n)-slot approximation flagged exact_supported=0. MPRA windows above 164 bp require `--exploratory-window`; the window has span=0 within truncation families, motivating a length-matched ranking model.

Observed gene labels, HEK293T ROSA26-4 D28 measurements, and predictions retain separate provenance. The 14-construct family-generalization record is FAIL, with same-tube technical repeats. CMVcore is the core fragment; CMV lacks human gene context. batch16/18 shared arms fell below validation criteria amid trainer/RNG/order differences.
