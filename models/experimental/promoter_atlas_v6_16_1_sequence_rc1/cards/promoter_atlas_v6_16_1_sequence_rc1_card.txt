## 中文

batch21 比较 164 bp K562 GSE118242 测量活性（log10）的损失函数。因本地数据可用而采用该数据。候选 GSE188639 只有 676 个 HEK293 核心启动子，低于 1000 条准入门槛，留作后续来源。

正反链 Hamming≤16 和精确重复构成 4710 连通分量；TRAIN/VAL/TEST 行数 9443/1180/1180，组数 2600/1090/1020，跨划分 Hamming≤16 配对为 0。

两臂×两 seed 共 4 次拟合，RTX5060 Laptop 单 GPU、batch32、AdamW lr0.0003/wd0.0001，最多 20 epochs，验证 Spearman patience5。总拟合 87.0s（约 1.45 GPU 分钟），预算 3 GPU 小时。两臂共享骨架、预处理、seed 和优化器；A 用 TRAIN 标准化活性的 MSE，B 加 `0.1*mean softplus(-sign(z_i-z_j)*(p_i-p_j))`，配对来自 minibatch 内 |z_i−z_j|≥0.5 的训练行。

| 配方 | TEST Spearman | MAE | R² | top10 精度 | 富集 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 0.6442 | 0.2157 | 0.4338 | 0.6186 | 6.19 |
| B | 0.6493 | 0.2169 | 0.4297 | 0.6102 | 6.10 |
| GC+3mer ridge | 0.3685 | 0.2912 | 0.1118 | 0.2458 | — |

TEST 为 1180 行、1020 组。预设要求 rho 增≥0.02、MAE 降≥5%、top10 持平或上升、两个 seed 均改善。实际 rho 增0.0051、MAE 降−0.56%，top10 和双 seed 条件未达成，IMPROVEMENT_MET=False。验证选择 B，结论为该排名损失未达到预设实用增益。结果适用于此 K562 164 bp 任务，新划分与 batch02 OOF0.79577 分别报告。

## English

batch21 compares losses for measured log10 K562 GSE118242 activity from 164 bp DNA. The dataset was selected for local availability. GSE188639 had 676 HEK293 core promoters, below the 1000-row entry threshold, and was retained as a future source.

Forward/reverse-complement Hamming≤16 and exact duplicates form 4710 components. TRAIN/VAL/TEST rows are 9443/1180/1180, groups 2600/1090/1020, and cross-split Hamming≤16 pairs zero.

Four fits cover two arms and two seeds on one RTX5060 Laptop GPU, batch32, AdamW lr0.0003/wd0.0001, up to 20 epochs, validation-Spearman patience5. Total fit time was 87.0s (about 1.45 GPU minutes), within a three-GPU-hour budget. Backbone, preprocessing, seed, and optimizer are matched. A uses MSE on TRAIN-standardized activity; B adds `0.1*mean softplus(-sign(z_i-z_j)*(p_i-p_j))` over training minibatch pairs with |z_i−z_j|≥0.5.

| Recipe | TEST Spearman | MAE | R² | Top10 precision | Enrichment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 0.6442 | 0.2157 | 0.4338 | 0.6186 | 6.19 |
| B | 0.6493 | 0.2169 | 0.4297 | 0.6102 | 6.10 |
| GC+3mer ridge | 0.3685 | 0.2912 | 0.1118 | 0.2458 | — |

TEST has 1180 rows and 1020 groups. Prespecified criteria require rho gain≥0.02, MAE reduction≥5%, stable/improved top10, and improvement in both seeds. Observed rho gain is 0.0051 and MAE reduction −0.56%; top10 and both-seed conditions failed, so IMPROVEMENT_MET=False. Validation selected B. The ranking loss fell short of the practical-gain rule. These results concern this K562 164 bp task and are reported separately from batch02 OOF0.79577 under its different split.
